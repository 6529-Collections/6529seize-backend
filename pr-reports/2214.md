# PR Report: Resolve reports for deleted posts

PR: https://github.com/6529-Collections/6529seize-backend/pull/2214
Branch: `agent-prxt/watchtower-deleted-reports` → `main`
Related PR: https://github.com/6529-Collections/6529seize-frontend/pull/4408

## Summary

WatchTower can explicitly close reports whose original post has been deleted,
without treating deletion as a moderator content-removal decision. Reports stop
contributing to the open queue and indicator, while saved evidence and reasoned
audit history remain available under their existing retention limits.

## What Changed

- Adds the versioned, idempotent `RESOLVE_DELETED_REPORT` action for authorized
  direct-session developers. It resolves all open reports for a missing
  published post as `RESOLVED_DELETED`, recording actor, reason and time.
- Rejects deletion resolution for live or edited posts. Mark reviewed retains
  its separate check-history meaning.
- Exposes deleted existence state and open-report count independently of
  moderation visibility, and includes deleted resolutions in resolved queues
  and counts.
- Preserves expired evidence deadlines during resolution and retention, and
  keeps sibling reports reviewed when outstanding assessments finish.

## Backend Impact

- Changed services: `api` for the action/contracts; `dbMigrationsLoop` for
  moderation retention. Required order: `dbMigrationsLoop`, then `api`, then
  the related frontend.
- API: additive action, report status and existence fields; generated models
  remain synchronized with the frontend.
- DB: existing varchar status fields; no schema change or data migration.
- No new services, configuration, queues or external integrations.

## Release Notes

Moderators can resolve deleted-post reports with an explicit reason. The
related frontend labels deleted originals and links directly to the reported
post instead of relying on links within its saved content.
