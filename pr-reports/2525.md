# PR Report: Daily TDH from the 6529 chain, and xTDH frozen with identities

PR: [Backend PR #2525](https://github.com/6529-Collections/6529seize-backend/pull/2525)
Branch: `claude/tdh-from-chain` → `claude/identity-freeze-flag` (the identity freeze PR), then `main`
Related PRs: the identity freeze PR #2512 it stacks on, which merges first; the frontend PR [6529seize-frontend#4995](https://github.com/6529-Collections/6529seize-frontend/pull/4995) "Show the TDH day from the 6529 Network, and freeze xTDH with identities".

## Summary

The 6529 chain's validators agree on each day's TDH. This PR lets 6529.io compare its own day with the chain's exactly and then read its TDH tables from the chain, behind two flags that are off by default. It also freezes xTDH at the identity freeze, so the site's xTDH matches what the chain imports.

This PR's commits are cacfa91bb, 90ef108c6, 3c8a4081a, c61969cad, 136b21809, 41c9bc3df, 2dab01977 and 7076a0216. The two commits below them, e4b253ae2 and c72cb5321, are the identity freeze PR's.

## What Changed

- **Compare tool.** `6529 run tdh:chain-compare` checks a chain day against 6529.io's exactly: wallets, identities, clusters, per-card values, roots and totals. It reads both sides with unauthenticated GETs only, and its exit code gives the verdict.
- **Chain-read path (`src/tdhChain`).** `tdhLoop`'s `chainPoll` events read the newest final day from the gateway and verify it: roots, counts, totals, clusters, and the cutoff block against Ethereum.
  - `FEATURE_TDH_CHAIN_SHADOW` compares the day and records it in `tdh_chain_days`.
  - `FEATURE_TDH_FROM_CHAIN` writes the TDH tables with the chain's values. The tables, `tdh_blocks`, the identities sync marker and the day's record commit in one transaction.
  - Follow-up steps run after the write, each marked and retried, and given up with an alert after 12 failures: NFT totals, history, metrics, the dated uploads, and the day files on Arweave.
- **Chain mode stops the old-path runs:** the 00:01 `tdhLoop` run, `tdhHistoryLoop`'s 00:30 run and the delegations loop's within-day rerun.
- **Old-path writes mark the chain day.** A write of a block the chain wrote sets that day's `superseded_at`, so `/api/blocks` says SITE again.
- **`/api/blocks`.** New fields `source`, `cycle_day` and `wallet_root`.
- **xTDH during the identity freeze:**
  - grant creation and changes answer 423 `IDENTITY_FROZEN`;
  - the universe recalculation and the grants review don't run;
  - reads are unchanged.
- **Docs.** `docs/architecture.md` has a new section, "TDH days from the 6529 chain".

## Backend Impact

- **New services:** none.
- **Changed services:**
  - `tdhLoop`: new `chainPoll` schedules;
  - `delegationsLoop`, `tdhHistoryLoop`;
  - `api`: `/api/blocks` and the xTDH grant routes;
  - `xTdhLoop`, `xTdhGrantsReviewerLoop`.
- **API:** `/api/blocks` gains `source` (always present), `cycle_day` and `wallet_root` (CHAIN days only). It is additive. The xTDH grant POSTs answer 423 during the freeze.
- **DB/entities/migrations:** new entity `tdh_chain_days`, synchronised by `dbMigrationsLoop`. No migration file, and no change to existing columns.
- **Queues/integrations:**
  - the gateway (`TDH_CHAIN_GATEWAY_URL`): unauthenticated GETs, one host;
  - the Ethereum RPC, for the cutoff block;
  - Arweave, for the day files.
  - No new queue. The "TDH calculations done" SNS follows a chain write as it follows a full `tdhLoop` run.

## Config / Env

- **The settings.** `FEATURE_TDH_CHAIN_SHADOW`, `FEATURE_TDH_FROM_CHAIN` and `TDH_CHAIN_GATEWAY_URL` go in the shared `prod/lambdas` secret. Both flags are off by default, and if both are on, chain mode wins.
- **No redeploy needed.** The settings are read again on every invocation of `tdhLoop`, `delegationsLoop` and `tdhHistoryLoop`.
- **xTDH** follows the identity freeze's `FEATURE_IDENTITY_FROZEN` and `IDENTITY_FREEZE_AT`; there is no second flag.
- **Rollback:**
  1. Turn `FEATURE_TDH_FROM_CHAIN` off.
  2. To recompute today's day at once, invoke `tdhLoop` with no event; its config already sets `TDH_RESET`. That marks the chain's day superseded.

## Risks / Follow-Ups

- **Turning chain mode on.** Do it only once shadow has recorded the first chain day, which shows as `SHADOW`, `INGESTED`, `MATCH` in `tdh_chain_days`. Without that row, the first chain poll writes that day itself and announces it a second time.
- **Quiet nights.** The ingest waits until the site's transaction index is 64 blocks past the cutoff, giving way three hours past midnight. With no transfers of the tracked collections after midnight, the day is written at about 03:05 instead of shortly after the chain applies it.
- **Keys too wide for the columns.** They are left out of a day and queued until the holdings PR's migration widens the columns. The day is then written and uploaded again.
- **Generated model.** `ApiBlockItem` was edited by hand because the generator couldn't run; regenerate it to confirm.
- **Gateway routes.** They are provisional (`/v1/tdh/days/...`) until the gateway publishes them.
