PR: [Backend PR #2525](https://github.com/6529-Collections/6529seize-backend/pull/2525)

Related PRs: the identity chain #2512 to #2518, on main and merged into this branch; the frontend PR [6529seize-frontend#4995](https://github.com/6529-Collections/6529seize-frontend/pull/4995) "Show the TDH day from the 6529 Network, and retire xTDH".

## Summary

The 6529 chain's validators agree on each day's TDH. This PR lets 6529.io compare its own day with the chain's exactly and then read its TDH tables from the chain, behind two flags that are off by default. 6529.io keeps computing its own day at 00:01 as the fallback, so its TDH is never missing or late whatever the chain does. It also retires xTDH with identities: the chain takes no xTDH and no grants over, so from the identity freeze grants can't be created or changed and xTDH is no longer recalculated.

## What Changed

- **Compare tool.** `6529 run tdh:chain-compare` checks a chain day against 6529.io's exactly: wallets, identities, clusters, per-card values, roots and totals. It reads both sides with unauthenticated GETs only, and its exit code gives the verdict. `--tool-commit` records the commit it runs from.
- **Chain-read path (`src/tdhChain`).** `tdhLoop`'s `chainPoll` events read the newest final day from the gateway and verify it: roots, counts, totals, clusters, and the cutoff block against Ethereum.
  - `FEATURE_TDH_CHAIN_SHADOW` compares the day and records it in `tdh_chain_days`. Its verdict is the comparison at the chain's cutoff block and grouping; the live tables' differences are recorded apart, split by whether a consolidation change since the cutoff explains them. A MISMATCH alerts, and so do live differences no grouping change explains and live tables that can't be compared. Nothing users see changes.
  - `FEATURE_TDH_FROM_CHAIN` writes the TDH tables with the chain's values, over the day 6529.io computed at 00:01 at the same midnight block. The tables, `tdh_blocks`, the identities sync marker and the day's record commit in one transaction.
  - Follow-up steps run after the write, each marked and retried, and given up with an alert after 12 failures: NFT totals, history, metrics, the dated uploads, and the day files on Arweave.
- **The fallback.** The 00:01 `tdhLoop` run and `tdhHistoryLoop`'s 00:30 run go on in every mode.
  - In chain mode the 00:01 day is interim. It is written and identities sync to it, but its dated uploads are held, and no votes are revoked and nothing is announced on its values. The chain's write replaces it, then uploads and announces. If that write lands while identities are being synced to the interim day, the sync holds the revocation and the announcement for the chain's generation to do.
  - After a rollback, a day still held as interim is published at the next poll.
  - With no usable chain day (the gateway down, no day served or not final, a list that doesn't rebuild its root, a failed check, a stalled chain), the site keeps its own day. From 4 hours past the boundary an alert fires once an hour, and the poller publishes the site's own day once: its uploads, then its announcement.
  - The 00:01 run leaves alone a day the chain already wrote, so a retry or a manual run can't undo it.
  - Chain mode stops only the delegations loop's within-day rerun, which would rewrite the chain's block.
- **Gateway client.** The published routes: `/v1/tdh`, `/v1/tdh/days/{day}`, and the day's `/wallets` and `/tokens` lists in chunks of up to 5,000 rows.
  - Every chunk must name the day's root and counts, and the roots rebuilt from the rows must equal the day's `walletRoot` and `tokenRoot`.
  - A gone pin, list or chunk (409, or 404 from a node without the lists) is read again at a fresh pin, three times at most.
  - A token list that stays 404, or that can't be read, leaves the day without tokens (MATCH_WITHOUT_TOKENS, logged).
  - A 404 "no such route" is a configuration error and fails the run.
- **Old-path writes mark the chain day.** A write of a block the chain wrote sets that day's `superseded_at`, so `/api/blocks` says SITE again.
- **`/api/blocks`.** New fields `source`, `cycle_day` and `wallet_root`.
- **xTDH retired** from the identity freeze, and for good from the switch-on:
  - grant creation and changes answer 423 `IDENTITY_FROZEN`, saying xTDH was retired;
  - the universe recalculation and the grants review don't run;
  - reads are unchanged.
  - Levels and the rating credit without xTDH from the switch-on are in the identity mirror PR #2516 (levels from the chain) and the switch-on PR #2518 (the identities sync stops writing levels; the credit is TDH alone). Nothing here adds xTDH to either.
- **Docs.** `docs/architecture.md` has a new section, "TDH days from the 6529 chain".

## Backend Impact

- **New services:** none.
- **Changed services:**
  - `tdhLoop`: new `chainPoll` schedules;
  - `delegationsLoop`, `tdhHistoryLoop`;
  - `api`: `/api/blocks` and the xTDH grant routes;
  - `xTdhLoop`, `xTdhGrantsReviewerLoop`.
- **API:** `/api/blocks` gains `source` (always present), `cycle_day` and `wallet_root` (CHAIN days only). It is additive. The xTDH grant POSTs answer 423 once xTDH is retired.
- **DB/entities/migrations:** new entity `tdh_chain_days`, synchronised by `dbMigrationsLoop`. No migration file, and no change to existing columns.
- **Queues/integrations:**
  - the gateway (`TDH_CHAIN_GATEWAY_URL`): unauthenticated GETs, one host, at most 10 a second from the poller and 2 a second from the compare tool;
  - the Ethereum RPC, for the cutoff block;
  - Arweave, for the day files.
  - No new queue. The "TDH calculations done" SNS follows the chain's write as it follows a full `tdhLoop` run. In chain mode the 00:01 day doesn't send it; the own-day point does, once, if the chain's day isn't written by then.

## Config / Env

- **The settings.** `FEATURE_TDH_CHAIN_SHADOW`, `FEATURE_TDH_FROM_CHAIN` and `TDH_CHAIN_GATEWAY_URL` go in the shared `prod/lambdas` secret. Both flags are off by default, and if both are on, chain mode wins.
- **The gateway URL.** `TDH_CHAIN_GATEWAY_URL=https://gateway.6529.io`, the same base URL as the identity mirror's `CHAIN_GATEWAY_URL`. The two gateway clients merge in a follow-up once both PRs land.
- **No redeploy needed.** The settings are read again on every invocation of `tdhLoop`, `delegationsLoop` and `tdhHistoryLoop`.
- **xTDH** is retired by the identity freeze: its own `FEATURE_IDENTITY_FROZEN` or `IDENTITY_FREEZE_AT`, or the switch-on's `FEATURE_IDENTITY_ON_CHAIN` or `IDENTITY_SWITCH_ON_AT`, which keeps it on for good; there is no flag of its own.
- **Rollback:**
  1. Turn `FEATURE_TDH_FROM_CHAIN` off.
  2. To recompute today's day at once, invoke `tdhLoop` with no event; its config already sets `TDH_RESET`. That marks the chain's day superseded.

## Risks / Follow-Ups

- **Turning chain mode on.** Do it only once shadow has recorded the first chain day as `SHADOW`, `INGESTED` in `tdh_chain_days`, with a MATCH or a MATCH_WITHOUT_TOKENS the team has accepted. That day was computed, uploaded and announced at 00:01 while the flag was off. Without the row, the first chain poll would write it again hours later and announce it again.
- **A chain day after the own-day point.** If the chain's day is written after the site's own day was published at the 4-hour point, it replaces that day: the day is uploaded and announced a second time.
- **Quiet nights.** The ingest waits until the site's transaction index is 64 blocks past the cutoff, giving way three hours past midnight. With no transfers of the tracked collections after midnight, the day is written at about 03:05 instead of shortly after the chain applies it. The site shows its own day meanwhile.
- **Keys too wide for the columns.** They are left out of a day and queued until the columns are wide enough; the day is then written and uploaded again. Main's columns are already 700 wide (#2522), enough for 16 wallets.
- **Generated model.** `ApiBlockItem` was edited by hand because the generator couldn't run; regenerate it to confirm.
- **Gateway routes.** The client reads the gateway's published TDH routes, the token route included (gateway release rc.26). Until a node serves a day's token list, a compare for it reads MATCH_WITHOUT_TOKENS.
