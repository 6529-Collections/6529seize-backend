# PR Report: Rebuild check: the holdings area's seed and rebuilders

PR: [#2551](https://github.com/6529-Collections/6529seize-backend/pull/2551)
Base: #2538's branch, with #2542's head merged in; merges after #2542 and #2538

## Summary

The rebuild check (#2542) listed the holdings area's seed and rebuilder as planned, since their code wasn't in its tree. #2528 and #2538 brought the rebuild steps. This PR adds what the check needs to run them: a seed that writes a small world on the check's private anvil and fills the holdings tables from it with the loops' own code, and the area's manifest entries. Nothing outside the rebuild check changes.

## What Changed

- **The seed** (`src/holdings/rebuild-seed/holdings-seed.ts`): Memes cards 1 to 4 (card 4 through a Manifold lazy claim), Meme Lab card 1 and NextGen collection 1 on the run's anvil. It also writes the tokens' metadata files into the run's seed files directory and then runs the same `rebuildHoldings` as the rebuilder.
- **Contract fixtures** (`SeedCreator.sol`, `SeedClaim.sol`, `SeedNextGen.sol`): stand-ins for Manifold's creator contract, Manifold's lazy claim extension and the NextGen core and minter. They keep only the signatures, events and view layout the loops read. They were written for this repository under its Apache-2.0 license, sit at the addresses in the repository's constants, and are loaded only by the seed. Their runtime code is built by the line in each header (solc 0.8.37, no metadata hash) and is reproducible byte for byte.
- **The manifest** (`src/rebuild/manifests/holdings.manifest.ts`):
  - the seed and the two rebuilders are no longer planned;
  - the NextGen and wallet transfer tables are rebuilt, and the cursors are rebuilt with their tables as INDEX;
  - write-time columns are volatile;
  - two area flags for the run;
  - every waiver states its reason.
- **One harness change** (`src/rebuild/manifests/probe-fixture.ts`): the upcoming card the subscription probe asks about moves from 4 to 5, since the seed mints card 4. No other seed touches card 5.

## Backend Impact

**Deploy:** none. No Lambda, route, schema, schedule, queue or alarm change; no loop, route or deploy imports these files.

**Live behaviour.** Unchanged.

**Other services.** None.

## Config / Env

None in any deployed environment. The rebuild check sets `DECENTRALIZED_MEDIA_FILES_URL` and `NEXTGEN_START_BLOCK_OVERRIDE` for its own run; both stay unset in production.

## Rollback

Revert the PR. Nothing to undo in data.

## Risks / Follow-Ups

- **What the check proves.** On its own it proves the rebuild is repeatable. That the rebuild equals the live loops is proven by `src/holdings/ethereum-index-rebuild.db.test.ts` (#2528) and `src/nextgen/nextgen-rebuild.db.test.ts` (#2538).
- **Meme ranks that read TDH.** The catalogue runs before TDH's rebuild and keeps the earlier ranks while `tdh_nft` is empty. The check doesn't cover the ranks read from TDH, and after a production rebuild they return with the NFT loop's next live run.
- **Dropping the fixtures.** A one-time recording of mainnet (`--source ethereum=replay:<dir>`, or an anvil state with the real contracts), made by the 6529.io team with a live RPC, replaces the seed's chain-writing half and the fixtures.
- **Meme Lab royalties** stay waived as unseeded until the team exports production's splits into `src/holdings/meme-lab-royalties.json`.
