# PR Report: Rebuild check: the mint tables rebuilt from the mint log

PR: [#2561](https://github.com/6529-Collections/6529seize-backend/pull/2561)
Base: #2536's branch (claude/mint-log), with #2542's head merged in; merges after #2542 and #2536

## Summary

The rebuild check (#2542) rebuilt one of the mint area's tables, the team list. #2536 brought the mint log and its rebuild from genesis files and logged writes. This PR runs that rebuild in the check: a seed that reaches the mint tables the way production does, through the genesis and the mint log, and the area's manifest entries. The check now rebuilds 22 of mint's 26 INDEX tables and compares them row by row. Nothing outside the rebuild check changes.

## What Changed

- **The seed** (`src/mint-log/mint-log-rebuild.seed.ts`, with `seed-collab.ts`, `seed-rows.ts` and `ids.ts` beside it):
  - card 1, a rememe and a royalties upload written before the log;
  - the genesis, with its files in the run's seed files;
  - logged writes through the minting writers with the area's flags on: cards 1 and 2, a rememe, uploads, two NextGen collections, and a Main Stage collaboration through the collaboration service;
  - its own entries closed into a segment in the records wave;
  - mint stats and the minted counts from the NFT and transactions loops.
- **The records fixture:** the personal area's values, copied. The records service and wave are written only when missing, and the seed closes only its own entries, so it runs with personal and publish in any order.
- **The chain's clock** (`mint-log-rebuild.chain-clock.ts`): mint stats are written once a mint has closed, and in the check a mint closes on the time of the run's mark block, or of the head block when the run names none, on the ethereum source. Production keeps the wall clock.
- **The rebuilders** (`src/mint-log/mint-log-rebuild-check.steps.ts`):
  - `mint.log` runs #2536's rebuild into empty tables, from the files source and the records wave;
  - `mint.stats` runs #2536's mint stats writer on the chain's clock;
  - `mint.announcements` is planned until the waves drop index is rebuilt.
- **The manifest** (`src/rebuild/manifests/mint.manifest.ts`):
  - the distribution loop's cursor becomes INDEX: the rebuild sets it, and a lost cursor would count every mint again;
  - new probes for NextGen proofs and burns, claim allowlists and proofs, wallet allocations, airdrops, action types and the collaboration;
  - rememes are probed sorted, without their refresh time;
  - every waiver states its reason.
