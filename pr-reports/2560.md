# PR Report: Rebuild check: the dm area replays a relay recording, checked event by event

PR: [#2560](https://github.com/6529-Collections/6529seize-backend/pull/2560)
Base: #2559's branch, with #2545's commits; merges after #2559 and #2545

## Summary

#2559 lets the rebuild check replay a recording of the relays, and counts a replay for `--final` only when every recorded event passes the signed-event check and its area's own check. This PR makes the encrypted-messages (dm) area the first to use it: a relay check for the e2ee streams, a seed that publishes the same events every run, the relay readers the dm steps sign in as, and a recording of the dm seed. Replayed, the dm area counts for a final run: 31 of 31 recorded events pass both checks. Nothing outside the rebuild check changes.

## What Changed

- **A relay check for the e2ee streams** (`src/e2ee/relay/e2ee-relay-check.ts`): `checkE2eeRelayItem` applies the rebuild's own check to a recorded event: the wave is one of the six e2ee families (`cv`, `dv`, `dr`, `bl`, `bk`, `md`), and the body hash, id and Ed25519 signature hold with the orderer's pinned key (`E2EE_ORDERER_PUBLIC_KEY`). It calls the existing `checkE2eeRecord` and changes nothing in it. A malformed event fails with a reason instead of throwing.
- **A seed that publishes the same events every run** (`src/rebuild/steps/e2ee-relay.determinism.ts`, `e2ee-relay.steps.ts`). While the dm seed runs, its own child process has:
  - a clock that starts at 2026-11-23 00:00 UTC and moves only when the seed moves it;
  - randomness from a generator seeded by a fixed label, behind `crypto.randomBytes`, `randomUUID`, `randomInt`, `randomFillSync`, `webcrypto.getRandomValues`, `generateKeyPairSync('x25519')` and `Math.random`, which covers the moderation seal's key, nonce and ephemeral key.

  After-commit work runs one callback at a time, so the moderation records don't race for the inbox's next position. Every stream is signed and published at the end, in name order, so each record's `seq` is fixed. The rebuilder applies retention at the seed's clock, and the seeded devices are valid for ten years, so a committed recording does not age.
- **The dm manifest** (`src/rebuild/manifests/dm.manifest.ts`): `relayChecks` names `checkE2eeRelayItem` for the six families, and `relayAccess` names the two grant wallets the dm steps sign in as: the orderer (the seed publishes as it) and the site reader (the rebuild reads every family as it).
- **The recording** (`src/rebuild/recordings/dm-relays`): the 31 records the dm seed published to the harness relay, their 4 payloads, the waves the run read and the relay's head, with the recorder's `PROVENANCE.json` and a `README.md` on how to replay it and record it again. `src/rebuild/recordings/.gitattributes` keeps its bytes exact on every checkout.

## Tests

- `e2ee-relay-check.test.ts` (7): the orderer's record passes in every family; another key's record, a changed body or signature, a wave outside the families, a malformed event and a missing flag fail with their reasons.
- `e2ee-relay.determinism.test.ts` (5): the same draws every run, sealed moderation records included; another label draws differently; a sealed record still opens with the moderation key; the clock moves only forward and only when told; the real clock and randomness come back afterwards.
- Two runs of the dm seed published the same 31 events and the same 4 payloads; before this change every event differed. Two recordings made one after the other match byte for byte apart from their `recorded_at` times.
- Replayed (`--area dm --source relays=replay:src/rebuild/recordings/dm-relays`), the dm area passes: 19 tables rebuilt and none differ; 20 probes, none differ or unproven; no source provisional; "final; 31 of 31 events pass the signed-event check and their area's".
- The e2ee and rebuild unit suites: 60 suites, 493 tests pass.

## Risks / Follow-Ups

- **A seed change means a new recording.** If the dm seed or the records it writes change, the replay fails with "differs from the recording"; the recording's `README.md` says how to record it again.
- **The API's probes run at the real time** against data stamped with the seed's clock. The seeded devices are valid for ten years, and no dm route the probes read filters by age.
- **The seed patches its own process.** The fixed clock and randomness last only while the dm seed runs, in its own child process; the API and the rebuilder keep the real ones.
