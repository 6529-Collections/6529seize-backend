# Drop Forge launch and EMMA processing

PR: [Backend #2143](https://github.com/6529-Collections/6529seize-backend/pull/2143)
Companion: [Frontend #4207](https://github.com/6529-Collections/6529seize-frontend/pull/4207)

EMMA Process and Finalize captures the clicking operator's authorized inputs,
processes ordered checkpoints and finalizes once. Refresh restores progress;
completed lists are read-only downloads. Expired authorization resumes the same
job; no service-account token is required.

Craft Launch queues initialization and opening airdrops immediately after
publication and preparation. Later phase updates/airdrops have independent
one-time schedules. Phase 2 and Public subscribers share the second gap; Public
configuration changes after Phase 2 closes. Each action checks readiness and
verified manual completion. Earlier ordinary failures do not prevent later groups.
Republished metadata queues Update Claim without reopening allocations.

Research runs at 17:15 Europe/Athens the next day, topping up to
`min(310, frozen Craft edition)` from fresh total supply. Craft/on-chain maximum
must agree, fixed allocations retain priority, and confirmed manual Research
receipts reduce the remaining top-up. Pay Artist remains manual.

`memesDistributionHandler` and `dropForgeLaunchHandler` replace the loop services.
They consume targeted SQS work; neither has a recurring one-minute trigger. A
request publishes under its SQL lock before committing its dispatch revision;
early delivery retries and failed publication rolls back the requested mutation.
Handlers establish one-time crash watchdogs before processing, queue outstanding
checkpoints/reports/receipts before acknowledgement, and stop between future
groups or after completion. Future groups are installed before opening work.
Standard-queue redelivery never substitutes for transaction idempotency.

Existing signer locking, persisted signed bytes/hash, exact recipient evidence,
canonical receipt/reorg checks and atomic settlement remain authoritative.
Authorized same-nonce speed-up/cancellation and reviewed remaining-allocation
recovery preserve uncertain nonce reservations. Duplicate/manual airdrops are
credited only with verified evidence; total supply and completion toggles alone
cannot prove delivery. Ordinary manual controls and automation share progress.
Every actual action success/failure/skip reports to the operations wave, with
transaction links and failure mentions through `@drops6529`.

Dedicated execution and Scheduler delivery role references live in versioned
stage configuration. No feature-specific GitHub role variables are needed.
Scoped deployment-policy supplements, queue DLQs/alarms, service catalog,
generated deployment/monitoring configuration and operator documentation are
updated. Deploy schema, Launch handler/queue/group, Distribution handler/queue,
API, then dependent frontend. Disable retired recurring rules and reconcile any
existing jobs/signed work before migrating; never reset allocation or nonce state.

Maintain [the setup guide](../docs/drop-forge-auto-launch-setup.md) with config,
IAM, reporting and rollout changes. It covers user-supplied waves/profiles, KMS,
funding/admin grants, deployment order, observation and DLQ redrive. Runtime flags
remain disabled by default. Internal operator procedures are kept out of the
expanded public help corpus; public phase windows and mention behavior remain.

The proposed IAM supplements require administrator review/application; they were
not applied or AWS-validated here. Live KMS/chain transactions, Scheduler/SQS
integration, production reporting and production-size EMMA workloads still need
the documented disabled/Sepolia rollout checks. Inclusion/finality can miss a
window; signed intent reconciliation continues beyond submission cutoffs.
